Petal Wheel Spin

Animation prototype. The inner wheel lights its 8 petals clockwise and lands on one. Each of the 16 outer petals breathes on its own slow cycle, and the whole ring lights up when you press Spin. It opens in a shimmer state where every petal twinkles softly. After landing, the whole flower blooms into the result's color, spreading out from the center. Tap the hub or press Spin.
